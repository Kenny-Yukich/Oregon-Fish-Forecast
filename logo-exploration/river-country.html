Three Sisters

Three shaded peaks, a nod to the Three Sisters that frame the Central Oregon pilot waters.

C5

Still Water

The peak and sun reflected in calm water across the lower body, broken by ripple lines.

Why it fits

It says “read the water,” which is the whole product. The ripple lines echo a river surface and the site’s “Know the water” tagline.

Watch-outs

The full-color reflection uses transparency; one-color versions use solid ripple bands. The small version omits the fine ripples.

Sunrise Bands

After rendering: C4, Canyon Country

C4 remains the strongest starting point. The warm canyon bands and blue river make it distinct within this set, and both the paper and reversed versions retain the chosen trout outline. The canyon scene also fits the Lower Deschutes launch brief.

  • Alpine alternative: C3 adds depth through three shaded peaks. C1 remains the original reference.
  • Round companion: C7 fills an avatar better. Its small version keeps a larger trout and removes fine landscape detail.
  • Small-size limit: at 16 px the fish-shaped marks read as silhouettes and color bands. Use the simplified icon export; the full landscape needs more room.
  • Before adopting: test a physical print and convert lockup text to outlines. Monochrome previews assume the shown paper or blue background.
